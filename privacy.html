VA to Z Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

VA to Z (the website at rrkennedy69.github.io/VA-to-Z and the VA to Z Chrome extension) helps veterans organize VA disability claims. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

What information the app handles

You may enter or import personal details (such as name, address, phone, email, date of birth, service history), health and disability information (conditions, ratings, symptoms, claim notes), documents you add, and an optional xAI API key. If you use VA.gov Sync, the extension reads your own profile, disability rating, claims, and service history from VA.gov while you are signed in there. It only reads; it never changes anything on VA.gov and never sees your VA.gov password.

Where your information is stored

All of it is stored only on your own device, in your browser's storage, and in any backup files, save folder, or (if you choose) your own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server, does not collect your data, and cannot see it. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking.

When information leaves your device

  • VA.gov Sync (optional): requests go directly from your browser to VA.gov using your own signed-in session, the same way VA.gov's own pages do.
  • AI helper (optional, off by default): if you turn it on and add your own xAI API key, the questions you ask and the claim and profile details you choose to include are sent directly from your browser to xAI (api.x.ai) to generate answers. xAI's own privacy policy applies to that data. Your Social Security number is never sent unless you turn on its separate setting.
  • Backups: backup files go only where you save them.
  • Cloud Backup to Google Drive (optional, website only, off unless you sign in): if you click "Sign in with Google," VA to Z asks Google for permission to use only a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. When you click "Back up now," your backup is sent directly from your browser to that hidden folder in your Drive. It never goes to the developer, and the developer has no access to your Google account or your Drive. Social Security numbers are left out of cloud backups unless you turn on the setting to include them. Google's privacy policy applies to data stored in your Google Drive. To remove it, click "Sign out" in the app and delete VA to Z's hidden app data in Google Drive (Settings > Manage apps), or revoke access in your Google Account.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit, or lending decisions.

Deleting your data

Use Tools > Clear All Data in the app, or remove the extension or clear your browser's site data. Delete any backup files you saved.
