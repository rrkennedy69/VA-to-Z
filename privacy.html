VA to Z Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

VA to Z (the website at rrkennedy69.github.io/VA-to-Z and the VA to Z Chrome extension) helps veterans organize VA disability claims. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

What information the app handles

You may enter or import personal details (such as name, address, phone, email, date of birth, service history), health and disability information (conditions, ratings, symptoms, claim notes), documents you add, and an optional xAI API key. If you use VA.gov Sync, the extension reads your own profile, disability rating, claims, and service history from VA.gov while you are signed in there. It only reads; it never changes anything on VA.gov and never sees your VA.gov password.

No medical records. VA to Z does not store, upload, back up, or send medical records. The Documents area accepts only non-medical paperwork: VA decision letters and code sheets, lay, buddy, and personal statements, DD-214 and service records, and hearing/appeal papers. There are no categories for service treatment records, VA or private treatment records, C&P exams, DBQs, nexus letters, test results, or C-files. The Profile no longer has a medical providers list (doctor or clinic names, addresses, phones, treatment dates, conditions treated) or VA health care enrollment / VA medical center fields. If you added medical records or provider info in an earlier version, the app shows a one-time notice that lets you download it and then removes it from the app; nothing is deleted silently. Until you do that, it is left out of backups, Google backup, and the AI, and older backups you restore skip it. Condition names, ratings, claim tracking, and your notes are still kept on your device; the notes and evidence log ask you not to paste medical records or treatment details.

Where your information is stored

All of it is stored only on your own device, in your browser's storage, and in any backup files, save folder, or (if you choose) your own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server, does not collect your data, and cannot see it. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking.

When information leaves your device

  • VA.gov Sync (optional): requests go directly from your browser to VA.gov using your own signed-in session, the same way VA.gov's own pages do.
  • AI helper (optional, off by default): if you turn it on and add your own xAI API key, the questions you ask and the claim and profile details you choose to include are sent directly from your browser to xAI (api.x.ai) to generate answers. xAI's own privacy policy applies to that data. Your Social Security number is never sent unless you turn on its separate setting.
  • Backups: backup files go only where you save them.
  • Automatic Google Drive backup (optional, website only, off unless you sign in): if you tap "Sign in with Google," VA to Z asks Google for permission to use only a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission) and to see your Google email address (used only on your device as a sign-in hint). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. After you sign in once, VA to Z automatically backs up your work to that folder a few seconds after you make changes and when you leave the page, replacing one backup file (va-to-z-backup.json). It goes directly from your browser to Google; it never goes to the developer. Your browser remembers only that you turned backup on and your email hint, never your Google password or access token; on later visits it quietly asks Google for a fresh short-lived access token, and if the browser blocks that, you see a "Tap to reconnect" button and your work keeps saving on your device in the meantime. On a new device, signing in brings your backup back (if that device already has data, you are asked first). Social Security numbers are left out of the Google backup unless you check the box to include them. To stop: choose "Turn off on this device" in Tools. To delete the backup: in Google Drive go to Settings → Manage apps → VA to Z → Delete hidden app data, and you can remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit, or lending decisions.

Deleting your data

Use Tools > Clear All Data in the app, or remove the extension or clear your browser's site data. Delete any backup files you saved.
