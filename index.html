Set up the AI helper (optional)

The lookup and the calculator (in Tools) work without this. The AI chat in My Claims needs a Grok API key from xAI.

  1. Go to console.x.ai (open it in your browser) and sign in or make an account.
  2. Add a payment method or credits under Billing. xAI charges for API use. Small chats usually cost pennies, but you are responsible for the bill.
  3. Open API Keys and click Create API Key. Give it a name like "VA Helper".
  4. Copy the key (it starts with xai-). You may only see it once.
  5. Paste it below and press Save, then Test.

Default is grok-4.7 (xAI's recommended chat model as of Oct 2026). If xAI renames models, type the new name here. See docs.x.ai/docs/models.

Privacy: your key is saved only in this browser's storage on this device. It is sent only to api.x.ai when you use the chat. Your claim notes are sent to xAI only when you chat. Anyone using this device and browser could see the key; use Clear All Data in Tools when you're done on a shared computer.

My Profile

Your profile is saved only on this device, in this browser. VA to Z has no servers, and no third party can see or store it. The only exception is if you turn on AI access below: then your profile is sent with your questions to xAI (Grok) to answer them.

Your profile is also included in backups you download in Tools, so keep backup files private. Fill in only what you want. These are details VA forms often ask for (21-526EZ, 21-0995, 21-10210, 21-4138, 21-686c), so you have them in one place. Tap a section to open it.

AI access to my profile

Off (the default): the AI helper in My Claims does not get your profile. It only gets that claim's rule text, checklist, notes, and your questions.

On: each time you chat, your profile is sent to xAI along with your question, so drafts can include your name, service dates, units, and so on.

Off by default. Most drafts don't need it; you can write it on the final form yourself.

Backup and Restore▾

What is a backup? It is one file (ending in .vaz) with everything you've entered here: your profile, claims, notes, checklists, AI chats, calculator, settings, and your documents. It is compressed and checked for damage when you restore it.

Why make one? Your work is saved only inside this browser on this device. It can be lost if you clear your browsing history, use private mode, or the browser cleans up storage. A backup also lets you move your work to a new phone or computer.

Make a new backup after big changes. Keep the file somewhere safe (like a USB drive or your personal cloud storage).

No backup saved yet on this device.

On a phone or tablet, a menu opens so you can pick where to save it (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, and more). On a computer, you may get a "Save As" window to choose a folder. Otherwise it goes to your Downloads folder.

Sync VA Data▾

Instead of typing everything in, you can copy your own info from VA.gov: your name, contact info, service history, VA ratings, and claims. A small free browser add-on (the VA to Z Sync extension) reads it while you're logged in to VA.gov and hands it to this app. You check every item before anything is changed.

It only reads. It never sees your password, never submits anything to VA, and your info goes straight from VA.gov to this app on your computer. It does not go to any other server.

Step 1: Install the extension (one time)

Computer only (Windows or Mac) in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. It does not work on phones or tablets.

  1. Download the extension (va-to-z-sync.zip).
  2. Open your Downloads folder. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All (Windows) or double-click it (Mac). You get a folder named va-to-z-sync. Move it somewhere you'll keep it, such as Documents. Don't delete it later, because the extension runs from that folder.
  3. In the address bar, type chrome://extensions (Chrome) or edge://extensions (Edge) and press Enter.
  4. Turn on Developer mode. In Chrome the switch is at the top right; in Edge it's on the left side.
  5. Click Load unpacked and select the va-to-z-sync folder you extracted.
  6. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin VA to Z Sync so its button is always visible.
  7. Using VA to Z saved on your computer (a file) instead of the website? On the extensions page click Details under VA to Z Sync and turn on Allow access to file URLs.

Sometimes Chrome or Edge shows a message such as "Disable developer mode extensions." That's normal for add-ons installed this way. Click the X or Keep, not Disable.

Step 2: Pull your info

  1. Go to VA.gov and sign in (Login.gov or ID.me) as usual.
  2. Click the VA to Z Sync button in your toolbar, then Pull my VA data.
  3. If this app is open in another tab, the info appears below for review. If not, click Download VA data file in the extension, then use Import VA data file here.
Clear All Data▾
